Our story & craftsmanship

Thrown by hand in our Portland studio

Every plate and bowl begins as a lump of locally sourced stoneware clay, centred and pulled on the wheel by Celine and a small team of potters. No moulds, no shortcuts.

Glazes mixed from scratch

Our signature sage, oat and rust glazes are mixed in-house from natural minerals. They're food-safe, lead-free, and finished to feel soft under your fingertips.

Made to be used, every single day

High-fired to 1,240°C, our pieces are dishwasher, microwave and oven safe. We design for the everyday table, not the display cabinet.
